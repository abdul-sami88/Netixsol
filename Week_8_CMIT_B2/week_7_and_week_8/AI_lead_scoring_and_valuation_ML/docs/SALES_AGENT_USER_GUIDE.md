## 1. How to Handle Inbound Leads (Lead Triage SLA)

Jab bhi naya client CRM ya website par aayega, AI system usko 3 categories mein daal deta hai:

| Priority Badge | Conversion Probability | Response Time SLA | Sales Agent Action Plan |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **🔥 Hot Lead** | **$\ge 70\%$** | **< 1 Hour** | **Top Priority!** Lead ko fauran call karein. Buyer ka budget verified hai aur wo site visit ke liye tayar hai. Deal close karne ke liye senior partner ko involve karein. |
| **🌤 Warm Lead** | **$40\% - 69\%$** | **< 24 Hours** | Lead genuine hai lekin koi objection hai (e.g. installment plan chahiye ya price par baat karni hai). WhatsApp par comparable properties share karein. |
| **❄️ Cold Lead** | **$< 40\%$** | **Automated Drip** | Manual calling mein time waste na karein. System inko automated WhatsApp newsletter aur weekly market updates bhejta rahega. |

## 2. Using Property Valuation in Price Negotiations

Jab seller property list karwana chahe, ya buyer offer dena chahe:

### The 80% Confidence Interval (Market Trading Range)
AI aap ko sirf ek single number nahi deta, balkay **Lower Bound**, **Median Price**, aur **Upper Bound** deta hai:
- **Fair Market Price (Median)**: Property ki bilkul fair trading value.
- **Lower Bound (P10)**: Agar seller ko emergency mein bechna ho ya buyer bargain dhoond raha ho.
- **Upper Bound (P90)**: Agar ghar brand new luxury condition mein ho.

### Investment Verdicts Explained:
1. **Underpriced**: Asking price lower bound se bhi kam hai.
   - *Agent Pitch*: *"Sir, yeh property market value se 8-10% sasti mil rahi hai, foran token money de kar lock karein!"*
2. **Fair Market Price**: Asking price lower aur upper bound ke darmiyan hai.
   - *Agent Pitch*: *"Seller ki demand market ke mutabiq bilkul fair hai. Normal 2-3% negotiation ke baad deal finalize ho sakti hai."*
3. **Overpriced**: Asking price upper bound se bhi zyada hai.
   - *Agent Pitch to Seller*: *"Sir, aap ki demand market se 15% zyada hai. Is price par ghar 6 maheenay tak nahi bikay ga. Realistic price PKR X Crore hai."*

## 3. Explaining SHAP Talking Points to Clients (UrduLish Scripts)

Tab 3 mein AI aap ko batata hai ke price kyun barhi ya kyun kam hui. Client se baat karte waqt in talking points ka use karein:

- **Location Premium**:  
  *"Sir, DHA Phase 6 mein main boulevard aur corner plot hone ki wajah se is ghar ki value mein normal plot se taqreeban 75 se 80 Lakh ka izafa shamil hai."*
- **Construction Age**:  
  *"Ghar sirf 3 saal purana hai, structure bilkul brand new condition mein hai jis se renovation ka 30-40 Lakh rupay bach jayega."*
- **Distance to Commercial**:  
  *"Commercial area 3 km door hone ki wajah se location quiet aur peaceful hai, family ke liye ideal environment hai."*
