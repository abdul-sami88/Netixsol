# Knowledge Base - RealEstate Hub Pakistan

## 1. Developer Profiles & Track Record

### Emaar Pakistan
- **Overview**: Emaar Pakistan is a subsidiary of global developer Emaar Properties (Dubai). Known for luxury beachfront living in Karachi (Crescent Bay) and high-end gated communities in Islamabad (Canyon Views).
- **Track Record**: 100% land ownership titles, zero litigation history, international architecture standards.
- **NOC Status**: Fully approved by SBCA (Sindh Building Control Authority) and CDA (Capital Development Authority).

### Bahria Town Pvt Ltd
- **Overview**: Asia's largest private real estate developer with signature master-planned communities in Lahore, Islamabad, and Karachi.
- **Key Features**: Independent 24/7 grid station power supply (zero load-shedding), international standard schools, hospitals, safari parks, and 18-hole golf courses.
- **NOC & Approval**: LDA approved (Bahria Town Lahore Sector A to F), RDA/CDA approved (Bahria Town Rawalpindi/Islamabad Phase 1-8).

### DHA (Defence Housing Authority)
- **Overview**: Pakistan's premier housing authority backed by constitutional legislative acts. Operating in Lahore, Islamabad/Rawalpindi, Karachi, Multan, Gujranwala, and Peshawar.
- **Key Advantages**: Unmatched capital appreciation, highest liquidity in real estate market, secure transfer procedure through official Armed Forces transfer offices.

## 2. Legal NOC & Property Transfer FAQs

### Transfer & Verification Process
- **Q**: Direct owner se transfer par kitna time lagta hai?
- **A**: Official DHA or Bahria transfer office mein buyer aur seller ki physical presence ke baad 3-5 working days mein Allotment / Transfer Letter issue ho jata hai.

### Overseas Buyers Guide
- **Q**: Overseas Pakistanis baghair visit kiye property kaise buy kar sakte hain?
- **A**: Overseas Pakistani High Commission / Embassy se Power of Attorney (PoA) attest karwa kar apne representative ke zariye transaction complete kar sakte hain. Payment Direct Roshan Digital Account (RDA) se pay ki ja sakti hai.

### Tax & FBR Regulations
- **Q**: Buyer aur Seller par kitna tax lagu hota hai?
- **A**: Active Filer ke liye Advance Income Tax (Section 236K) 3% hai, jabki Non-Filer ke liye 12% tak hota hai. Stamp duty aur Town tax local authority rules ke mutabiq 1% se 2% hoti hai.

## 3. General Installment & Payment Policies

- **Upfront Cash Discount**: Full upfront lump-sum payment par 10% se 15% flat discount offer hota hai.
- **Possession-Linked Payment**: 50% payment complete hone par physical possession handed over kar di jati hai, remaining 50% easy monthly installments mein pay ki ja sakti hai.
- **Default & Penalty Rules**: 3 consecutive monthly installment delay par 2% monthly sur-charge hota hai. Client written request par payment plan extend karwa sakta hai.
