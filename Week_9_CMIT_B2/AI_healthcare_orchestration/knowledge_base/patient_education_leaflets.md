# City Care Clinics - Patient Education & Home Care Leaflets

## 1. Hypertension & High Blood Pressure Care (BP aur Dil ki Hifazat)
- **Target Range:** For most adults, blood pressure should remain below **130/80 mmHg** (or below 140/90 mmHg for elderly individuals as advised by their cardiologist).
- **Dietary Sodium Reduction:** Limit daily salt intake to less than 1 level teaspoon (approx. 5g) per day. Avoid pickles (achaar), papad, salted nuts, chips, and canned processed soups.
- **Physical Activity:** Aim for at least 30 minutes of moderate aerobic exercise (brisk walking, cycling) 5 days per week.
- **Home BP Monitoring Protocol:**
  - Rest quietly for 5 minutes before taking a reading.
  - Sit with back supported and arm resting at heart level.
  - Do not smoke, drink tea, or exercise 30 minutes before measurement.
  - Record morning (before breakfast) and evening (before dinner) readings in a logbook.
- **Warning Signs Requiring Urgent Care:** BP exceeding 180/110 mmHg, severe occipital headache, blurred vision, chest heaviness, or shortness of breath.

## 2. Type 2 Diabetes Self-Management & Rule of 15 (Sugar aur Hypoglycemia)
- **Target Blood Glucose:**
  - Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS): **80 – 130 mg/dL**
  - Post-prandial (2 hours after meal): Less than **180 mg/dL**
  - HbA1c Target: Generally **< 7.0%** for non-pregnant adults.
- **Hypoglycemia (Low Blood Sugar) Warning Signs:**
  - Symptoms: Trembling/shakiness, cold sweats, sudden intense hunger, palpitations, dizziness, confusion.
  - **The "Rule of 15":** If blood sugar drops below 70 mg/dL, immediately consume 15 grams of fast-acting simple carbohydrates (3 glucose tablets, 1/2 cup of fruit juice, or 3 teaspoons of sugar/honey dissolved in water). Wait 15 minutes and re-test.
- **Foot Care Essentials:** Inspect feet daily for blisters, cuts, or redness. Never walk barefoot, even indoors. Dry thoroughly between toes after washing.

## 3. Dengue Fever Warning Signs & Hydration (Dengue Bukhar aur Disprin/Brufen Mana)
- **Endemic Context:** Dengue is prevalent across urban Pakistan (especially post-monsoon from August to November in Lahore and Rawalpindi/Islamabad).
- **Core Treatment Pillar:** Oral rehydration is the primary management. Patients must consume **2.5 to 3 liters** of fluids daily (ORS/Nimkol, coconut water, fresh soup/broth, diluted juices, plain water).
- **Critical Medication Prohibition:** **STRICTLY NEVER TAKE ASPIRIN (Disprin), IBUPROFEN (Brufen), OR DICLOFENAC (Voltral)** during suspected or confirmed Dengue fever! These NSAIDs aggravate bleeding tendencies and induce acute thrombocytopenia. Only use **Paracetamol (Panadol)** for fever, up to a maximum of 4000mg per day in adults.
- **Warning Signs (Critical Phase 3-7 Days):**
  - Severe persistent abdominal pain or tenderness.
  - Persistent vomiting (unable to keep fluids down).
  - Bleeding from gums, nose, or blood in stool/vomit.
  - Extreme fatigue, restlessness, or sudden lethargy.
  - Rapid drop in platelet count (< 50,000 /mcL) or sudden rise in hematocrit (HCT).
  - *If any warning sign appears, transfer immediately to an emergency hospital.*

## 4. Bronchial Asthma & Inhaler Technique (Dama aur Inhaler ka Tariqa)
- **Controller vs. Reliever Inhalers:**
  - **Reliever (Blue Inhaler e.g. Ventolin / Salbutamol):** Use 1-2 puffs immediately for acute wheezing, chest tightness, or coughing attacks.
  - **Controller / Preventer (e.g. Seretide, Symbicort):** Must be used every single day as prescribed (morning and evening), even when feeling completely healthy, to prevent airway inflammation.
- **Inhaler Spacer Advice:** Always use a spacer device with metered-dose inhalers (MDIs) to maximize drug delivery into deep lungs and reduce oral thrush.
- **Trigger Management:** Avoid passive cigarette smoke, uncleaned carpets, sudden cold night air, and burning mosquito coils. Rinse mouth with water after using steroid inhalers.

## 5. Pediatric Gastroenteritis & Nimkol WHO-ORS (Bachay ke liye Nimkol sachet kitne paani mein banate hain)
- **Primary Danger:** Dehydration is the main threat in vomiting and diarrhea in infants and toddlers.
- **Preparation of WHO-ORS (Bachay ke liye Nimkol sachet banana):** Dissolve 1 complete sachet into exactly **1 liter (approx. 4 glasses)** of clean boiled and cooled drinking water. Do not mix in milk, juice, or boiling water. Use within 24 hours of preparation.
- **Administration Technique:** Give small frequent sips (1 teaspoon every 2-3 minutes) using a spoon or syringe. If the child vomits, pause for 10 minutes, then resume at a slower rate.
- **Zinc Supplementation:** Children aged 6 months and older should receive 20mg elemental zinc daily for 14 days to reduce diarrheal duration and prevent recurrence.
- **Danger Signs in Children:** Sunken eyes, depressed soft spot on head (fontanelle), absent tears when crying, no wet diapers/urination for over 6 hours, or excessive sleepiness/unresponsiveness.
