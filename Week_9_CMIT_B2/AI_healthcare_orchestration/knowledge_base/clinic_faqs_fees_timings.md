## 2. Consultation Fees by Clinical Specialty
All consultation fees are regulated and standard across all 5 branches:
- **General Medicine / Family Medicine:** PKR 2,000 – PKR 2,500
- **Paediatrics (Child Health & Neonatology):** PKR 2,000 – PKR 2,500
- **Gynaecology & Obstetrics:** PKR 2,500 – PKR 3,000
- **Cardiology (Adult Heart Care):** PKR 3,000 – PKR 3,500
- **Dermatology & Skin Care:** PKR 2,500 – PKR 3,000
- **ENT (Ear, Nose, Throat):** PKR 2,000 – PKR 2,500
- **Follow-up Consultations:** Within 7 days of initial visit for the same complaint are charged at 50% discount (or free if solely for brief lab report review).

## 3. Appointment Policies, Rescheduling & Cancellations
- **Arrival Time:** Patients are advised to arrive 15 minutes before their scheduled appointment slot for vitals measurement.
- **Grace Period:** If a patient is late by more than 15 minutes, their token may be assigned to the next waiting patient, and they will be accommodated in the next open slot.
- **Cancellation & Rescheduling:** Patients can reschedule or cancel without any fee penalty up to 2 hours prior to the appointment via WhatsApp, our Web portal, or telephone.
- **Walk-in Policy:** Walk-ins are accommodated based on doctor slot availability; priority is always given to pre-booked appointments and triaged urgent cases.

## 4. Payment Methods & Insurance
- **Accepted Modes:** Cash, Debit/Credit Cards (Visa / Mastercard / PayPak), JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and Raast instant bank transfer.

## 5. Emergency Care Protocol
- **Disclaimer:** City Care Clinics are outpatient ambulatory clinics and do NOT operate a 24/7 Level-1 trauma center or catheterization laboratory.
- **Immediate Escalation:** If a patient experiences severe chest pain, sudden stroke signs (FAST), severe breathing difficulty, active massive bleeding, or unresponsiveness, staff and AI assistants immediately direct the patient to call **Rescue 1122** or proceed directly to the nearest emergency room (PIC/Mayo Hospital in Lahore; PIMS/Shifa in Islamabad).
