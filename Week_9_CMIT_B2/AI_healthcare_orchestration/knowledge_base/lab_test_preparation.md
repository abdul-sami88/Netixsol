# City Care Clinics - Laboratory Test Preparation & Sampling Instructions

## 1. Fasting Blood Glucose (FBS) & Oral Glucose Tolerance Test (OGTT)
- **Fasting Requirement:** Strict fasting for **8 to 10 hours** prior to the blood draw.
- **Allowed:** Plain water is permitted and encouraged to maintain hydration.
- **Forbidden:** No tea, coffee, milk, juices, chewing gum, smoking, or breakfast before sample collection.
- **Medication Rule:** Do NOT take morning diabetes tablets (e.g. Metformin, Glimepiride) or morning insulin until after the fasting blood draw is complete.
- **OGTT Procedure:** After the fasting sample, the patient drinks a 75g glucose solution provided by the lab. Blood samples are drawn exactly 1 hour and 2 hours post-ingestion. The patient must remain seated in the clinic waiting area and refrain from walking around or consuming food during the 2-hour testing window.

---

## 2. Lipid Profile (Cholesterol, HDL, LDL, Triglycerides)
- **Fasting Requirement:** Strict overnight fasting for **10 to 12 hours** (maximum 14 hours).
- **Dietary Precaution:** Avoid heavy fatty meals, fried food, or alcohol for 24 hours prior to testing.
- **Hydration:** Plain drinking water is allowed.
- **Statins & Medications:** Regular cholesterol-lowering medicines (e.g. Atorvastatin / Lipitor, Rosuvastatin) should be taken as normally scheduled with plain water unless specifically instructed otherwise by the physician.

---

## 3. Complete Blood Count (CBC) & ESR
- **Fasting Requirement:** **No fasting required**. The test can be performed at any time of day, before or after meals.
- **Hydration:** Drink plenty of water before testing to ensure good venous access.
- **Special Instruction:** If testing during suspected Dengue or viral infection, repeat testing is typically scheduled at the same time daily (e.g. every 24 hours) to monitor platelet trends accurately.

---

## 4. Glycated Hemoglobin (HbA1c)
- **Fasting Requirement:** **No fasting required**. HbA1c measures average blood sugar over the previous 2 to 3 months and is not affected by recent meals or day-to-day blood glucose fluctuations.
- **Timing:** Can be drawn at any hour of the day.

---

## 5. Liver Function Tests (LFT) & Renal Function Tests (RFT / KFT)
- **Fasting Requirement:** Fasting of **6 to 8 hours** is preferred for LFT (especially for serum bilirubin and alkaline phosphatase accuracy). RFT (Serum Creatinine and Blood Urea) can be drawn without fasting, but 6-hour fasting is optimal if combined with a comprehensive metabolic panel.
- **Hydration:** Normal plain water is allowed.

---

## 6. Thyroid Function Panel (TSH, Free T3, Free T4)
- **Fasting Requirement:** Fasting is not strictly required, but early morning sampling (between 08:00 AM and 10:00 AM) is recommended due to diurnal TSH variation.
- **Thyroid Medication Rule:** If taking Levothyroxine (e.g. Thyroxine 25/50/100mcg), **skip the morning tablet before the blood draw**. Take the prescribed dose immediately after the blood sample is collected.

---

## 7. Abdominal & Pelvic Ultrasound Preparation (Ultrasound karwane ki tayyari / Full Bladder)
- **Upper Abdomen Ultrasound (Liver, Gallbladder, Pancreas, Spleen):**
  - **Fasting:** Strict fasting for **6 to 8 hours** prior to the scan to ensure gallbladder distension and reduce overlying bowel gas. Avoid dairy or fatty foods on the morning of the exam.
- **Pelvic Ultrasound / KUB (Kidneys, Ureters, Bladder, Gynaecology / Pelvic scan karwana):**
  - **Full Bladder Requirement (Pani peena aur bladder bharna):** Patient must drink 4 to 6 glasses (approx. 1 liter) of plain water 1 hour before the examination and **must NOT urinate** until the scan is completed. An adequately distended urinary bladder acts as an acoustic window.

---

## 8. 24-Hour Urine Protein / Creatinine Collection
- **Procedure:**
  - Day 1 (Start): Wake up at 07:00 AM and discard the first morning urine into the toilet. Note the time.
  - Collect all subsequent urine passed throughout the day and night into the specialized preservative container provided by the clinic laboratory.
  - Day 2 (Finish): Exactly at 07:00 AM on the second morning, urinate into the container. This completes the 24-hour collection.
- **Storage:** Keep the urine container in a cool place or domestic refrigerator (do NOT freeze) during the 24-hour collection period. Bring container to the lab within 2 hours of completion.
